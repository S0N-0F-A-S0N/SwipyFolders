## 2024-11-11 - Clarifying Timeouts & Alert Text
**Learning:** Hardcoded text settings and alerts can be slightly awkward (like "Not enabled" vs "Disabled", or missing units for time variables like double tap timeouts) in iOS tweak preference bundles.
**Action:** Always check the settings pane UI values (like floats being formatted without units) and standard alerts for simple UI/UX micro-polish when editing tweak preferences.
